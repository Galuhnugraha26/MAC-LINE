# Code of Conduct

MAC-LINE welcomes constructive technical collaboration. Please:

- Be respectful and inclusive; criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Provide reproducible evidence and accept reasonable review feedback.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, spam, or publish personal information.
- Do not fabricate test results, benchmark evidence, or authorship.
- Keep disagreement professional and relevant to project scope.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, and restrict participation when necessary. Security-sensitive information should be handled using `SECURITY.md`, not posted publicly.
